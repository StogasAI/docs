  allowStoredCredentials or requireTwoFactor needs organization management.
  displayName is your name in this organization. With allowAccessTokens false,
  members' access tokens get 403 ACCESS_TOKENS_NOT_ALLOWED for this organization
  at once. allowPlaygroundAssistant lets members turn on the Playground
  assistant, which reads the organization as them and sends what it reads to the
  model through their own API key. With allowStoredCredentials false, the
  organization keeps only provider credentials that its own encryption keys
  encrypt: adding a stored credential gets 409 STORED_CREDENTIALS_NOT_ALLOWED,
  and turning it off gets 409 STORED_CREDENTIALS_EXIST while any stored
  credential remains. With requireTwoFactor true, a member whose account has no
  passkey or authenticator app gets 403 TWO_FACTOR_REQUIRED for this
  organization, unless its identity provider signed the session in or authorized
  the access token. Turning it on needs a passkey or an authenticator app on
  your own account, and gets 409 TWO_FACTOR_MISSING without one."
