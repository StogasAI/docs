title: Single sign-on
description: Sign members in through your identity provider, and let people at your domains join.

---

An external organization can connect one OpenID Connect identity provider, such as Okta, Microsoft
Entra ID or Google Workspace. Members can then sign in through it, the organization can require
that, and people at its verified domains can join through a single sign-on invite. SAML isn't
supported. Set it up in **Settings → Single sign-on**, which needs **Organization → Manage**.

## Register Stogas with your provider

Create a web application that uses the authorization code flow, with the redirect URI that
**Settings** shows. Sign-in asks for the `openid`, `email` and `profile` scopes, or only the first
two when the provider's discovery document lists its scopes without `profile`.

The issuer's OpenID Connect discovery document must name exactly that issuer. A multi-tenant
endpoint, such as Microsoft's `common`, lets any tenant sign in, so it is refused. For Microsoft
Entra ID, use `https://login.microsoftonline.com/<tenant-id>/v2.0`.

## Domains

A domain stays verified, so you can remove its TXT record once it is. When another organization
verifies the same domain with its own record, the domain moves to that organization.

## Accounts

Single sign-on is another way to sign in to a Stogas account, and every account has a password,
including accounts created through single sign-on. Leaving the organization or disconnecting the
provider therefore never locks anyone out.

An account links to its person by the provider's subject identifier, never the email, which some
providers let people change. A provider that gives each client its own identifiers keeps the links
only while the client ID stays the same.

A member's [picture](/docs/members#pictures) comes from the provider's `picture` claim when it
names a JPEG or WebP image over HTTPS. Microsoft Entra ID sends none, so its members upload their
own.

Stogas stores the issuer, the client ID, the client secret encrypted, and each linked member's
subject identifier, last sign-in and any picture taken from the provider. [Activity](/docs/activity)
records each change to single sign-on and each member who joins or links through it, never the
secret.
