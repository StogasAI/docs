Usage is prepaid unless your organization has an [invoiced billing](#invoiced-billing) agreement.
Requests spend your organization's credit at the [catalog](https://stogas.ai/catalog) rates. A new organization needs at least $1 of confirmed
credit, after fees, before it can send requests.

## Add credit

<Steps>
<Step>

### Save billing details

Select the organization that receives the credit and save its [billing details](#billing-details).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a payment method

On **Balance**, choose [Bitcoin or Monero](#pay-with-bitcoin-or-monero), or **Choose currency**
for [CCPayment](#pay-through-ccpayment). Select the coin and network, then check the estimated
fees and USD credit.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send the payment

Select **Deposit address** and send from your wallet.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the credit

**Recent payments** shows when the credit is confirmed.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Fees

| Charge                           | Fee                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Adding credit                    | 2%, plus the payment processor, network and conversion costs. Each is listed separately. |
| Requests with managed providers  | The catalog rates. No other fee.                                                         |
| Requests with [BYOK](/docs/byok) | 2% of the usage at catalog rates. Your provider bills you for the usage.                 |

Your wallet's sending fee is separate. The BYOK fee uses catalog rates even if you pay your
provider a negotiated rate.

Each request [holds](/docs/usage#how-a-request-is-charged) its largest possible cost, then charges
its actual usage. If a request's final record cannot be recovered, contact
[billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai).

## Billing details

Each organization has its own billing details. Save them before you pay: each payment keeps the
details saved when we received it, and later changes do not change earlier payments or
receipts.

- Choose **Individual** or **Business**, and add your country, state and ZIP code. We sell only
  in the 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia; see [Availability](/docs/availability).
- You can add a name and street address. A business can also add a billing email and a purchase
  order reference, which appear on its receipts and invoices.

## Pay with Bitcoin or Monero

We receive Bitcoin and Monero directly, without a payment processor. Each member of an
organization gets their own deposit address for each coin. You can save it and reuse it for later
payments.

Scan the QR code, select **Open wallet**, or copy the address. Send only the selected coin, on its
own blockchain, and check the address and amount in your wallet.

- Any amount is credited at the price and network cost when we receive it, after the 2% fee and,
  for Monero, a 0.4% conversion fee. A payment smaller than the network cost, or a Monero payment
  below 0.0001 XMR, adds no credit.
- Credit is added after 2 Bitcoin confirmations, about 20 minutes, or 30 Monero confirmations,
  about an hour.
- If a payment is missing, contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai) with its coin and
  transaction ID.

## Pay through CCPayment

Choose a coin and network, then enter the USD credit that you want. The calculator estimates the
coin amount and each fee. There is no payment deadline.

- Copy the amount separately, because the QR code contains only the address.
- Include the memo or tag when one is shown, and send at least the processor's minimum. A wrong
  network or a missing memo can make a payment impossible to recover.
- Each payment is credited at its price when received, after the actual processor fee, the network
  and conversion costs shown, and the 2% fee. A late payment or a different amount is still
  credited.

## Prepaid limits

U.S. rules for prepaid balances limit how much credit a prepaid organization can take:

- Its balance, plus what it spent in the last 24 hours, can't exceed $2,000.
- Each member's payments can add up to $10,000 of credit in 24 hours, across all their
  organizations. Each member gets their own crypto deposit address, and a payment counts toward
  the member whose address it was sent to.

Credit above a limit stays pending and is added as the limits allow: 24 hours after the
organization spends, or as the member's earlier payments pass 24 hours. **Balance** shows the
pending credit and when more is available, and the amount box tells you before you pay. Pending
credit belongs to the organization, like its balance. For higher limits, contact
[ops@stogas.ai](mailto:ops@stogas.ai). [Invoiced billing](#invoiced-billing) has no prepaid limits.

## Payment status

**Recent payments** on the **Balance** page lists each payment with its status. Select a status
with an icon to see more.

| Status              | Meaning                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Confirming**      | We see the payment and are waiting for network confirmations.                                                                                                |
| **Crediting**       | The payment is confirmed, and we are adding the credit.                                                                                                      |
| **Credited**        | The credit is on your balance.                                                                                                                               |
| **Partly credited** | Part of the credit is on your balance, and the rest is pending under the [prepaid limits](#prepaid-limits).                                                  |
| **Pending**         | The payment is confirmed, and its credit is pending under the [prepaid limits](#prepaid-limits).                                                             |
| **Under review**    | We or CCPayment are checking the payment. If CCPayment holds it, contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai); another payment does not release it. |
| **Failed**          | The payment did not complete on the network, so we added no credit.                                                                                          |
| **Too small**       | The payment was smaller than the fees to receive it, so we added no credit.                                                                                  |

## Receipts

Once a payment's credit, or its first part, is on your balance, select **Receipt** in its row.
The PDF lists the AI credit, our fee, network costs and any conversion fee, with your billing
details, the dates, the exchange rate and the transaction reference. It shows cents; an attached
record keeps the exact amounts. The receipt number and details stay the same when you download it
again.

## Invoiced billing

An organization with a signed agreement can pay for its usage by invoice. To set one up, save the
organization's [billing details](#billing-details) and contact
[billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai).

- Requests can spend until the balance reaches minus the agreed credit limit. **Credit left** on
  the **Balance** page shows how much more they can spend.
- On the 1st of each month, we issue an invoice for the previous month's usage. Months follow UTC.
  **Not yet invoiced** shows the usage that the next invoice will bill.
- An invoice is due after the agreed payment terms, such as 30 days. **Add funds** starts at the
  amount due, and you can pay it with any payment method. The usual [fees](#fees) for adding credit
  apply, and payments apply to the oldest invoice first.
- **Invoices** on the **Balance** page lists each invoice with its status and PDF. Members who can
  view billing can download invoices; members who manage billing can pay them.
- If an invoice stays overdue, we can end the agreement. The organization then returns to prepaid
  billing, new requests fail while its balance is below zero, and its invoices stay due. **Add
  funds** then starts at everything the balance owes plus $10, so requests can run again.
- Until the organization pays what it owes and the agreement ends, you cannot delete it, and its
  last member cannot leave it or delete their account.

| Status          | Meaning                                                     |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Open**        | The invoice is not paid and not yet due.                    |
| **Partly paid** | Payments cover part of the invoice, and it is not yet due.  |
| **Overdue**     | The due date has passed, and part of the invoice is unpaid. |
| **Paid**        | Payments cover the whole invoice.                           |

## Refunds

You cannot withdraw prepaid credit. Refunds follow the [Terms of Service](https://stogas.ai/tos)
and the law. Contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai) about payment errors or refunds.
