title: Balance and billing

On **Balance**, choose Bitcoin or Monero and select **Deposit address**. The estimate shows how much to send for your desired USD credit, including estimated fees. Your final credit uses the exchange rate and fees recorded for the payment.

## Making a payment

Scan the QR code, select **Open wallet**, or copy the address. The QR code and wallet link include the current estimated amount when available. Your wallet's sending fee is separate.

Send only the selected currency on its native blockchain. Check the address and amount in your wallet before sending.

Opening deposit details again returns the same address for your user and currency. Addresses expire after 24 hours; opening the dialog during the final hour gets a replacement. Always open the current deposit details before sending, rather than saving an address for recurring payments.

**Recent payments** shows received payments and their credit status. Credit is added after confirmation. If a payment is missing or needs review, contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai) with its currency and transaction reference. Never send wallet keys or recovery phrases.

## Billing details

Each organization has its own billing details. Choose **Individual** or **Business** and a country before opening a deposit address. For the United States, add a state and ZIP code; for Canada, add a province or territory and postal code. You may add a name and postal address. Businesses can also add a billing email, tax ID, and purchase-order reference. A saved tax ID does not establish tax exemption or verified business status.

Save billing changes before paying. Each payment keeps the details saved when it was received, even when several payments use the same address. Updating billing details does not replace the address or change earlier payments and receipts.

## Receipts

Once a payment is settled and credited, select **Receipt** in its payment row. The PDF itemizes prepaid AI credit, the Stogas fee, network costs, and any currency-conversion fee. It includes your saved billing details, payment and issue dates, the exchange rate, and the transaction reference.

USD amounts display cents; an attached record preserves exact amounts and payment evidence. The receipt number and customer details stay fixed when downloaded again. Tax collected records the amount actually collected; it does not establish tax exemption.

## Refunds

Prepaid credit is not withdrawable. Refunds follow the [Terms of Service](https://stogas.ai/tos) and applicable law. Contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai) for payment errors or refund requests.
