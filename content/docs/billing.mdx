Usage is prepaid unless your organization has an [invoiced billing](#invoiced-billing) agreement.
Requests spend your organization's credit at the [catalog](https://stogas.ai/catalog) rates, and
each request [holds](/docs/usage#how-a-request-is-charged) its largest possible cost until it
settles.

A new organization becomes active with its first $1 of credit after fees, or with an invoiced
billing agreement, and stays active. Until then, you can only add credit, manage billing and leave
it.

## Fees

| Charge                           | Fee                                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Adding credit                    | 2%, plus the payment processor, network and conversion costs             |
| Requests with managed providers  | The catalog rates                                                        |
| Requests with [BYOK](/docs/byok) | 2% of the usage at catalog rates. Your provider bills you for the usage. |

## Payment methods

We receive Bitcoin and Monero directly. Other coins and networks go through
[CCPayment](https://ccpayment.com). A payment is credited at its price when we receive it, less
these costs and then the 2% fee.

| Method    | Credited after                    | Costs                                 | Smallest payment                          |
| --------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Bitcoin   | 2 confirmations, about 20 minutes | Network cost                          | More than the network cost                |
| Monero    | 30 confirmations, about an hour   | Network cost, then 0.4% conversion    | 0.0001 XMR and more than the network cost |
| CCPayment | CCPayment confirms it             | Processor fee, network and conversion | The processor's minimum                   |

- Each member gets their own Bitcoin and Monero deposit addresses, which they can reuse.
- If our price sources disagree by more than 3% on a payment's price, we check that price before
  we credit it.
- CCPayment has no payment deadline, and credits a late payment or a different amount. A payment
  on the wrong network or without its memo may be lost.

## Billing details

Save your organization's billing details before you pay. Each receipt keeps the details saved when
we received its payment. We sell only in the 50 U.S. states and the District of Columbia, as
[Availability](/docs/availability) explains.

Receipt PDFs show cents and attach a JSON record with the exact amounts and exchange rates. A
receipt keeps its number and details when you download it again.

## Invoiced billing

An organization with a signed agreement can pay for its usage by invoice, with no prepaid limits.
To set one up, contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai).

- Requests can spend until the balance reaches minus the agreed credit limit.
- On the 1st of each month, we invoice the previous month's usage. Months follow UTC.
- An invoice is due after the agreed payment terms, such as 30 days. You can pay it with any
  payment method, the usual [fees](#fees) apply, and payments go to the oldest invoice first.
- A paid invoice has a receipt that lists the payments that paid it. A payment's receipt shows
  what its credit paid of each invoice and of usage not invoiced yet.
- If we take back a payment that an invoice receipt lists, such as for a review, downloading that
  receipt again gives a new one that names the receipt it replaces.
- If an invoice stays overdue, we can end the agreement. The organization then returns to prepaid
  billing, new requests fail while its balance is below zero, and its invoices stay due.
- Until the organization pays what it owes and the agreement ends, you cannot delete it, and its
  last member cannot leave it or delete their account.

## Balance changes

Balance changes are every change to your organization's credit except spending, which
[usage](/docs/usage) covers:

| Change              | When                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Payment**         | A payment's credit reaches the balance.                                                 |
| **Credit released** | More of a payment's credit reaches the balance, after a limit held it back or a review. |
| **Held for review** | A payment goes to review, and its credit leaves the balance until the review ends.      |
| **Refund**          | We refund a payment.                                                                    |
| **Sales tax**       | We collect tax on kept credit when invoiced billing ends, or return it when it starts.  |
| **Adjustment**      | We correct the balance.                                                                 |

**Export** downloads the changes that match your filters as CSV or NDJSON, at most 50,000 of them.
[Activity](/docs/activity) records each export.

## Billing emails

If a business saves a billing email, we send it only these emails, each with its PDF, from
[billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai):

| Email                | When we send it                                                             |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Invoice**          | We issue an invoice.                                                        |
| **Payment reminder** | 3 days before an unpaid invoice is due, on payment terms of 7 days or more. |
| **Overdue notice**   | An unpaid invoice becomes overdue.                                          |
| **Receipt**          | A payment's credit reaches your balance.                                    |

Reply to any of them to reach us. To stop them, remove the billing email.

## Refunds

You cannot withdraw prepaid credit. Refunds follow the [Terms of Service](https://stogas.ai/tos)
and the law. Contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai) about a refund or a missing
payment, with its coin and transaction ID.
