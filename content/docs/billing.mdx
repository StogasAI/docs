Usage is prepaid. Requests spend your organization's credit at the
[catalog](https://stogas.ai/catalog) rates. A new organization needs at least $1 of confirmed
credit, after fees, before it can send requests.

## Add credit

<Steps>
<Step>

### Save billing details

Select the organization that receives the credit and save its [billing details](#billing-details).

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose a payment method

On **Balance**, choose [Bitcoin or Monero](#pay-with-bitcoin-or-monero), or **Choose currency**
for [CCPayment](#pay-through-ccpayment). Select the coin and network, then check the estimated
fees and USD credit.

</Step>
<Step>

### Send the payment

Select **Deposit address** and send from your wallet.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the credit

**Recent payments** shows when the credit is confirmed.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Fees

| Charge                           | Fee                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Adding credit                    | 2%, plus the payment processor, network and conversion costs. Each is listed separately. |
| Requests with managed providers  | The catalog rates. No other fee.                                                         |
| Requests with [BYOK](/docs/byok) | 2% of the usage at catalog rates. Your provider bills you for the usage.                 |

Your wallet's sending fee is separate. The BYOK fee uses catalog rates even if you pay your
provider a negotiated rate.

Each request [holds](/docs/usage#how-a-request-is-charged) its largest possible cost, then charges
its actual usage. If a request's final record cannot be recovered, contact
[billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai).

## Billing details

Each organization has its own billing details. Save them before you pay: each payment keeps the
details saved when we received it, and later changes do not change earlier payments or
receipts.

- Choose **Individual** or **Business**, and a country. [Sanctioned countries](/docs/availability)
  are not accepted.
- In the United States, add a state and ZIP code. In Canada, add a province or territory and a
  postal code.
- You can add a name and postal address. A business can also add a billing email, tax ID and
  purchase order reference. A tax ID does not make you tax exempt.

## Pay with Bitcoin or Monero

We receive Bitcoin and Monero directly, without a payment processor. Each organization has one
deposit address for each coin. You can save it and reuse it for later payments.

Scan the QR code, select **Open wallet**, or copy the address. Send only the selected coin, on its
own blockchain, and check the address and amount in your wallet.

- Any amount is credited at the price and network cost when we receive it, after the 2% fee and,
  for Monero, a 0.4% conversion fee. A payment smaller than the network cost, or a Monero payment
  below 0.0001 XMR, adds no credit.
- Credit is added after 2 Bitcoin confirmations, about 20 minutes, or 30 Monero confirmations,
  about an hour.
- If a payment is missing, contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai) with its coin and
  transaction ID.

## Pay through CCPayment

Choose a coin and network, then enter the USD credit that you want. The calculator estimates the
coin amount and each fee. There is no payment deadline.

- Copy the amount separately, because the QR code contains only the address.
- Include the memo or tag when one is shown, and send at least the processor's minimum. A wrong
  network or a missing memo can make a payment impossible to recover.
- Each payment is credited at its price when received, after the actual processor fee, the network
  and conversion costs shown, and the 2% fee. A late payment or a different amount is still
  credited.

## Payment status

**Recent payments** on the **Balance** page lists each payment with its status. Select a status
with a warning icon to see what happened.

| Status           | Meaning                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Confirming**   | We see the payment and are waiting for network confirmations.                                                                                                |
| **Crediting**    | The payment is confirmed, and we are adding the credit.                                                                                                      |
| **Credited**     | The credit is on your balance.                                                                                                                               |
| **Under review** | We or CCPayment are checking the payment. If CCPayment holds it, contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai); another payment does not release it. |
| **Failed**       | The payment did not complete on the network, so we added no credit.                                                                                          |
| **Too small**    | The payment was smaller than the fees to receive it, so we added no credit.                                                                                  |

## Receipts

After a payment is credited, select **Receipt** in its row. The PDF lists the AI credit, our
fee, network costs and any conversion fee, with your billing details, the dates, the exchange rate
and the transaction reference. It shows cents; an attached record keeps the exact amounts. The
receipt number and details stay the same when you download it again.

## Refunds

You cannot withdraw prepaid credit. Refunds follow the [Terms of Service](https://stogas.ai/tos)
and the law. Contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai) about payment errors or refunds.
