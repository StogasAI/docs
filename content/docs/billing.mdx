Usage is prepaid unless your organization has an [invoiced billing](#invoiced-billing) agreement.
Requests spend your organization's credit at the [catalog](https://stogas.ai/catalog) rates, and
each request [holds](/docs/usage#how-a-request-is-charged) its largest possible cost until it
settles.

A new organization becomes active with its first $1 of credit after fees, or with an invoiced
billing agreement, and stays active. Until then, you can only add credit, manage billing and leave
it.

## Fees

| Charge                           | Fee                                                                      |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Adding credit                    | 2%, plus the payment processor, network and conversion costs             |
| Requests with managed providers  | The catalog rates                                                        |
| Requests with [BYOK](/docs/byok) | 2% of the usage at catalog rates. Your provider bills you for the usage. |

## Payment methods

We receive Bitcoin and Monero directly, and other coins through [CCPayment](https://ccpayment.com).

- A Bitcoin or Monero payment smaller than the fees to receive it is not credited.
- Each member has their own Bitcoin and Monero deposit addresses, which they can use again.
- If our price sources disagree by more than 3% on a payment's price, we check that price before
  we credit it.
- CCPayment has no payment deadline, and credits a late payment or a different amount. A payment
  on the wrong network or without its memo may be lost.
- A receipt keeps the billing details saved when we received its payment, and its PDF attaches a
  JSON record with the exact amounts and exchange rates.

## Invoiced billing

An organization with a signed agreement can pay for its usage by invoice, with no prepaid limits.
To set one up, contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai).

- Requests can spend until the balance reaches minus the agreed credit limit.
- On the 1st of each month, we invoice the previous month's usage. Months follow UTC.
- An invoice is due after the payment terms in your agreement, such as 30 days after we issue it.
- You can pay invoices with any payment method, and the usual [fees](#fees) apply. A payment pays
  the oldest unpaid invoice first.
- If an invoice stays overdue, we can end the agreement. The organization then returns to prepaid
  billing, new requests fail while its balance is below zero, and its invoices stay due.
- Until the agreement ends and you pay every invoice, you cannot delete the organization, and its
  last member cannot leave it or delete their account. Leaving fails with
  `409 ORG_INVOICED_BILLING`. We issue the last invoice on the 1st after the agreement ends.

## Balance changes

Balance changes are every change to your organization's credit except spending, which
[usage](/docs/usage) covers:

| Change              | When                                                                                    |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Payment**         | A payment's credit reaches the balance.                                                 |
| **Credit released** | More of a payment's credit reaches the balance, after a limit held it back or a review. |
| **Held for review** | A payment goes to review, and its credit leaves the balance until the review ends.      |
| **Refund**          | We refund a payment.                                                                    |
| **Sales tax**       | We collect tax on kept credit when invoiced billing ends, or return it when it starts.  |
| **Adjustment**      | We correct the balance.                                                                 |

## Billing emails

A saved billing email gets each invoice and receipt with its PDF, a reminder 3 days before an
unpaid invoice is due on payment terms of 7 days or more, and a notice when an invoice becomes
overdue. Reply to any of them to reach us.

## Refunds

You cannot withdraw prepaid credit. Refunds follow the [Terms of Service](https://stogas.ai/tos)
and the law. Contact [billing@stogas.ai](mailto:billing@stogas.ai) about a refund or a missing
payment, with its coin and transaction ID.
