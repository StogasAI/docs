A grant lets selected members create their own [member keys](/docs/api-keys#member-keys). Each key
carries its owner's identity into request history, so you can see who used what. A grant has no
secret and cannot call the API. For an application that must keep running without a member, use a
[service key](/docs/api-keys#service-keys).

Grants exist in external organizations, and their names are unique regardless of case.
[Give a teammate API access](/docs/members#give-a-teammate-api-access) shows the full setup.

## Allowances and access

A member who holds **Use** on the grant or its folder can create keys under it, up to the grant's
allowance. The role needs no organization-wide permissions. Lowering the allowance deletes no keys,
and the member cannot create another key until they are below it.

<Callout type="warn">
	When a member loses their last **Use** on a grant, we delete their keys under it. Use from another
	role, the folder or the organization keeps the keys. Restoring access does not bring deleted keys
	back.
</Callout>

Disabling a grant rejects new requests from all its keys at once and keeps the keys.

## Policies and shared limits

A grant's policy applies to every key under it, together with the
[other policies](/docs/policies/scopes#which-policies-apply) of a member key. A grant's shared
limits count requests from all its keys, and each key can also have its own
[limits](/docs/policies/limits).
