A grant lets selected members create their own [member keys](/docs/api-keys#member-keys). Each key
carries its owner's identity into request history, so you can see who used what. A grant has no
secret and cannot call the API. For an application that must keep running without a member, use a
[service key](/docs/api-keys#service-keys).

Grants are available in external organizations. For the full setup, see
[Give a teammate API access](/docs/members#give-a-teammate-api-access).

## Create a grant

Open **Grants** and create a grant. It sets:

- how many keys each assigned member can create,
- whether the grant is enabled,
- whether members can edit their keys' policies.

Each member has their own allowance. Lowering it does not delete keys; the member cannot create
another key until they are below the new allowance.

## Assign a grant

In **Members → Roles**, add the grant or its folder to a role with **Use**, then assign the role to
members. The role needs no organization-wide permissions. Members then create keys under the grant
from **API keys** when they need them.

<Callout type="warn">
	When a member loses their last **Use** on a grant, we delete their keys under it. Use from another
	role, the folder or the organization keeps the keys. Restoring access does not bring deleted keys
	back. The dashboard warns you before a removal deletes keys.
</Callout>

See the [permission reference](/docs/members#permission-reference) for View, Use, Manage and Admin.

## Disable or delete a grant

Disabling a grant rejects new requests from all its keys at once and keeps the keys. Use it for a
pause you can undo. Deleting a grant deletes every key under it; the dashboard shows how many
before you confirm.

Select several grants to enable, disable or delete them together. **Select all matching** includes
every grant you can manage that matches the current filter, up to 100 at a time in the list's order.

## Policies and shared limits

A member-key request must pass the policies of the organization, folder, grant, owning member, each
of that member's roles, the key and the selected provider credential. A grant's shared limits count
requests from all its keys; each key can also have its own limits. See
[Scopes and inheritance](/docs/policies/scopes) and [Limits](/docs/policies/limits).

## Track use

On **API keys**, filter by grant or member to see their keys. In [Analytics](/docs/analytics),
filter by `grant:` or `member:` to see their requests and cost.
