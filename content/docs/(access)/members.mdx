---
title: Members and roles
description: Manage organization membership, assign permissions and connect roles to grants.
---

Open **Members** in an external organization to manage its members, roles and invitations. Roles
grant organization permissions and can be assigned View, Use, Manage, or Admin on particular folders, grants and
service keys or provider credentials. A [grant](/docs/grants) separately controls which member keys a person may create.
Personal organizations have no collaborative roles, grants or invitations.

## Invite a member

Create an invitation from the **Invites** tab. Choose its expiry and permitted uses,
then share the invitation link. Delete an unused invitation to stop further joins; this does
not remove members who already joined. Joining assigns the organization's current default role.

## Assign roles

Create a role from the **Roles** tab and select its permissions. Assign roles from the member
list, individually or to a selection. Keep administrative permissions limited to the people
who need them. The role list shows the permissions and member count directly.

A role can have no organization-wide permissions and still grant access to assigned resources.
Adding a member gives them all of that role's resource rights, including management; removing the
role removes that source of access. Use on a grant or its folder lets members create their own keys.

Member managers can grant organization permissions they hold. New API-key or credential rights also
require Admin at the matching scope, including organization-wide grants. Existing recipient access
may be preserved.
Role-management permission allows broader delegation. The final role manager cannot leave or be
removed while another member remains.

## Connect roles to grants

Link a role on the **Grants** page. Members with that role can use the grant, subject to their
permissions and its key allowance. A direct member assignment remains independent of their roles.
The grant's [assignment rules](/docs/grants#create-and-assign-a-grant) own what happens when access
changes.

## Remove access

Removing a member's last route to a grant deletes their member keys under that grant. Another
role, direct assignment, folder, or organization permission that grants Use preserves those keys. Restoring the role does not restore
deleted keys. Review the affected keys before confirming a role or membership removal.

For a reversible pause, disable the grant or an individual API key instead. Removing service-key
access preserves the key. Rotate its secret to revoke copies they may hold.

Voluntary departure preserves outstanding invitations. Administrative removal revokes invitations
created by that member. The last member's departure deletes the organization and its active resources;
financial history is retained.
