A personal organization is for your own work and has no members, roles or grants. An external
organization has members, and roles give them organization-wide permissions, and **View**, **Use**,
**Manage** or **Admin** on selected folders, grants, service keys and provider credentials. A
[grant](/docs/grants) controls which member keys a person can create.

## Give a teammate API access

1. Choose or create a [grant](/docs/grants) with suitable limits.
2. Choose or create a role with **Use** on that grant or its folder. It needs no organization-wide
   API key permissions.
3. Invite the teammate and assign the role. Their default role and any other roles also apply.
4. The teammate creates their own key under the grant.

Inviting and assigning roles needs **Members → Manage**. Assigning a role that gives grant access
also needs **Admin** on that grant, its folder or the organization. Creating or editing a role needs
**Roles → Manage**.

## Invitations

Each member and each unused use of an invitation holds a seat. An active organization has 3 seats
by default, plus one for each $100 it has added to its balance in total, and a seat stays used up
after its member leaves or is removed. For more seats, email [ops@stogas.ai](mailto:ops@stogas.ai).

Joining assigns the organization's current default role, so creating or changing an invitation also
needs the permissions to assign that role. An invitation link has at most 30 uses. A
[single sign-on](/docs/single-sign-on) invite has up to 1,000 uses and no link: people at your
verified domains join through it when they sign in through your identity provider. An organization
has at most one, and it gains uses only while a domain is verified and the identity provider has
passed its test.

An invitation is deleted when its last use is taken or it expires. Until then, you can change its
name, uses and expiry. It needs at least one unused use, added uses need free seats, and a new
expiry is 1 minute to 30 days from the time you change it. Deleting an invitation stops further
joins, and members who already joined stay.

## Pictures

A member's picture comes from your [identity provider](/docs/single-sign-on) until they upload one,
and a removed picture stays removed until the member uploads another.

Stogas keeps only a square image of at most 128 pixels a side, encoded again from the image's
pixels, so no metadata such as a camera's location stays with it.

## Roles

- A member gets the combined access of all their roles.
- A role with no organization-wide permissions can still give access to the resources it lists.
- Members can assign organization-wide permissions they hold. Giving new access to API keys or
  credentials also needs **Admin** on the matching resource.
- Taking a role from someone, or removing them, needs the same permissions as assigning that role.
  You can drop your own roles.
- **Roles → Manage** can create roles with permissions the manager does not hold, so give it only
  to people you trust with full control.
- At least one member always holds **Roles → Manage**. A change that would leave none fails with
  `409 ORG_LAST_ROLE_MANAGER`, so the last member who holds it cannot leave or be removed while
  other members remain.

## Remove access

Removing a member's last **Use** on a grant deletes their keys under it, as
[Grants](/docs/grants) explains. To pause access without deleting anything, disable the grant or
the key instead. Removing someone's access to a service key keeps the key, so rotate its secret to
stop copies they may hold.

A member's invitations stay valid after they leave, and removing a member revokes the invitations
they created. When the last member leaves, the organization is deleted, and its financial history
is kept.

## Permission reference

Permissions decide what a member can do, and [policies](/docs/policies/scopes) on their roles can
still narrow their requests. [Who can edit each policy](/docs/policies/editing#who-can-edit) also
depends on the parent policy's delegation settings.

### Resource permissions

Add a folder, grant, service key or provider credential to a role and choose:

| Permission | Dashboard | Allows                                                                                           |
| ---------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `view`     | View      | Read settings, policies and usage.                                                               |
| `use`      | Use       | View, plus reveal a service-key secret, create member keys from a grant, or assign a credential. |
| `manage`   | Manage    | View, plus configure, rotate, disable or delete the resource.                                    |
| `access`   | Admin     | Use and Manage, plus give others access within that resource.                                    |

Use and Manage can be combined as **Use and manage**. Manage alone cannot reveal a secret or give
access to someone else.

- Only the owner can see a member key's secret and send requests with it. Managers can change its
  settings.
- Credentials are not in folders. To add one to a key, see
  [Order credentials](/docs/byok#order-credentials).

### Organization permissions

| Permission               | Dashboard             | Allows                                                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------ | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `activity.read`          | Activity → Read       | Read and export [Activity](/docs/activity).                                                                                                                        |
| `analytics.read_own`     | Analytics → Read own  | Read the requests of your own member keys.                                                                                                                         |
| `analytics.read_all`     | Analytics → Read all  | Read every request in the organization.                                                                                                                            |
| `api_keys.read_all`      | API keys → Read all   | Read every API key, grant and folder, every policy except credential policies, and every request.                                                                  |
| `api_keys.use_all`       | API keys → Use all    | Reveal every service-key secret, and create your own keys from any grant.                                                                                          |
| `api_keys.manage_all`    | API keys → Manage all | Create, edit and delete every API key, grant and folder, and edit every policy except credential policies.                                                         |
| `api_keys.manage_access` | API keys → Admin all  | Use and manage every API key, grant and folder, and give others access to them.                                                                                    |
| `billing.read`           | Billing → Read        | Read the balance, payments, invoices, receipts and billing details.                                                                                                |
| `billing.manage`         | Billing → Manage      | Save billing details, and get the payment addresses that add credit.                                                                                               |
| `byok.read_all`          | BYOK → Read all       | Read every provider credential and its policy.                                                                                                                     |
| `byok.use_all`           | BYOK → Use all        | Assign any provider credential to the keys you manage.                                                                                                             |
| `byok.manage_all`        | BYOK → Manage all     | Create, edit, replace, disable and delete every provider credential, and edit its policy.                                                                          |
| `byok.manage_access`     | BYOK → Admin all      | Use and manage every provider credential, and give others access to it.                                                                                            |
| `members.read`           | Members → Read        | Read members and roles.                                                                                                                                            |
| `members.manage`         | Members → Manage      | Invite and remove members, read, change and delete invitations, assign the roles that the [rules](#roles) allow, and remove any member's picture.                  |
| `organization.manage`    | Organization → Manage | Change the organization's name, picture and settings, and set up single sign-on. Members with it open the organization without single sign-on when it is required. |
| `roles.manage`           | Roles → Manage        | Create, edit and delete roles, including roles with permissions you do not hold. With `members.manage`, assign any role.                                           |

A role also gets the permissions that its permissions include. In API keys and BYOK, Admin all
includes Use all and Manage all, and each of those includes Read all. Billing → Manage includes
Billing → Read, Analytics → Read all includes Read own, and Members → Manage and Roles → Manage
include Members → Read.
